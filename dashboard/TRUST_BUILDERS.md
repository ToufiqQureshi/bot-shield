## Implementation Notes

### Design Principles
- Minimal icons (only where they add value)
- Typography-first hierarchy
- Asymmetric layouts (not everything in grids)

- Restrained interactions

- Real-world examples over hypothetical
- Technical depth without jargon
- Business impact alongside technical features
